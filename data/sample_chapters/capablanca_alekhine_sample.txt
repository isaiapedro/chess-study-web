Chapter 1: New York 1927

In this chapter we study a famous struggle: Alekhine vs Capablanca, New York 1927.
The game illustrates Capablanca's calm defense and the conversion of a structural edge.

1. d4 Nf6 Capablanca invites an Indian setup. 2. c4 e6 3. Nf3 b6 The Queen's Indian idea:
Black fianchettoes and contests e4. 4. g3 Bb7 5. Bg2 c5 A modern Benoni-flavored break.
6. d5 Capablanca accepts a central space wedge. After the middlegame complications,
watch how Black's pieces coordinate on the long diagonal while White's knight wanders.

Later Capablanca vs Alekhine, Capablanca - Alekhine World Championship, 1927 also shows
similar pawn-structure themes with hanging pawns and minority ideas.
